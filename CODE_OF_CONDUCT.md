# Code of Conduct

EEG Workbench is used by students, researchers and clinicians doing research, and it is built by people from many backgrounds and levels of experience. We want everyone to feel able to ask questions, make mistakes and contribute.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to EEG, to the web stack or to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and kind.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear before assuming the worst.
- Accept that people have different views and different levels of experience.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
- Publishing other people's private information, including recordings or data that could identify a person.
- Anything else that would reasonably make someone feel unwelcome or unsafe in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to the project maintainers using the contact details on their GitHub profiles. Reports are handled in confidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and temporarily or permanently ban people who do not follow this code. We will tell the person concerned what was reported and why we acted.
